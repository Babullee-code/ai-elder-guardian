中暑之前，先一步察覺。 Catch heatstroke before it starts.

結合居家感測器、智慧手錶與個人化 AI，日夜留意長者的身體與環境。一出現異狀，手錶先提醒；需要降溫時，自動開冷氣；風險升高，家人立刻收到通知。

Home sensors, a smartwatch and an AI that knows each person’s normal keep watch day and night. At the first sign of trouble the watch nudges them, the air conditioner turns on when cooling is needed, and family is alerted as soon as risk rises.

為什麼需要Why it matters

熱傷害，常發生在沒人注意的時候Heat illness often strikes when no one is watching

長者對熱與口渴的感覺比較遲鈍，也常為了省電不開冷氣。家人無法隨時在旁，等到出現明顯症狀，往往已經需要送醫。我們把應對提前到身體剛出現變化的時候。

Older adults feel heat and thirst less sharply, and many skip the air conditioner to save on electricity. Family can’t be there every hour, and by the time symptoms are obvious, a hospital visit is often already needed. We move the response up to the first change in the body.

熟睡的夜晚Asleep on a hot night

房間慢慢變熱，人在睡夢中很難察覺。A bedroom heats up slowly, and a sleeping person rarely notices.

心率持續偏高時，自動打開臥室冷氣。When heart rate stays high, the bedroom AC turns on.

獨自在家Home alone

身邊沒有人，早期徵兆沒人發現。With no one around, the early signs go unnoticed.

風險升高時依序通知家人，並說明原因。As risk rises, family is alerted in order, with the reasons.

外出途中Out in the midday heat

中午走路辦事，離涼爽的地方很遠。A midday errand on foot, far from anywhere cool.

提醒休息補水，必要時導航到最近的避暑地點。A prompt to rest and drink, and if needed, directions to the nearest cooling spot.

運作方式How it works

監測、預測、引導Monitor, predict, guide

家中每個房間放一個小型感測節點，長者戴一支市售智慧手錶。所有資料送到同一台雲端伺服器，合併分析後，再把結果送回手錶、家人與冷氣。

Each room gets a small sensor node, and the elder wears an off-the-shelf smartwatch. Everything goes to one cloud server, which combines and analyzes it, then sends results back to the watch, the family and the air conditioner.

  1. 1

    監測Monitor

    房間節點每 10 秒量一次溫度、濕度、有沒有人、冷氣有沒有開，陽台節點量戶外溫濕度。手錶量心率、活動狀態與 GPS，支援的錶款再加上皮膚溫度。

    Room nodes read temperature, humidity, presence and whether the AC is running every 10 seconds, and a balcony node reads outdoor conditions. The watch tracks heart rate, activity and GPS, plus skin temperature on models that support it.

    • 溫濕度Temp & humidity
    • 人體存在Presence
    • 冷氣運轉AC running
    • 心率Heart rate
    • 活動狀態Activity
    • GPS
  2. 2

    預測Predict

    伺服器每分鐘把各房間與手錶的資料對齊成一條時間軸，拿長者自己的基準值來比：先排除運動，再看數值能不能自己恢復，最後決定風險等級。

    Every minute, the server lines up room and watch data into one timeline and compares it with the person’s own baseline. It rules out exercise first, waits to see whether the numbers recover, then sets the risk level.

    • 個人基準值Personal baseline
    • 活動判斷Activity check
    • 恢復觀察Recovery watch
    • 熱島門檻Heat-island thresholds
  3. 3

    引導Guide

    依風險等級逐步行動，從手錶輕震、補水提醒，到自動開冷氣、通知家人、導航到最近的避暑地點。

    Actions step up with the risk level, from a gentle watch vibration and a reminder to drink water to switching on the AC, alerting family and giving directions to the nearest cooling spot.

    • 手錶震動Watch vibration
    • 自動開冷氣AC on
    • 通知家人Family alerts
    • 避暑導航Cooling-spot directions

風險分級 · 規則 v1Risk levels · rule v1

三級風險，應對逐級累加Three levels, and the actions add up

每升一級，都保留前一級的行動，再多加一層保護。數值連續 15 分鐘恢復正常，才會一級一級降回來。

Each level keeps everything the level below does and adds more. Levels step back down one at a time, and only after the numbers stay normal for 15 minutes.

輕度Mild

自我降溫Self-cooling

何時WHEN環境偏熱、心率持續高於個人基準，而且不是在運動。It’s hot, heart rate stays above the personal baseline, and exercise doesn’t explain it.

  • 手錶震動提醒The watch vibrates
  • 提示補水、休息A prompt to drink water and rest
  • 若正在睡覺，自動開冷氣If they’re asleep, the AC turns on
中度Moderate

環境降溫Environmental cooling

何時WHEN輕度處理 30 分鐘後仍未改善，或心率高出基準 25 bpm 以上。Mild risk hasn’t improved 30 minutes after its actions, or heart rate is 25 bpm or more above baseline.

輕度的所有行動，再加上：Everything at mild, plus:

  • 通知家人，並說明原因Family is alerted, with the reasons
  • 在室內：開冷氣Indoors: the AC turns on
  • 在戶外：導航到最近的避暑地點Outdoors: directions to the nearest cooling spot
高度High

第三方介入Third-party help

何時WHEN中度處理 20 分鐘後仍未改善，或長者對提醒沒有回應、也沒有動作。Moderate risk hasn’t improved 20 minutes after its actions, or the elder doesn’t respond to prompts and isn’t moving.

中度的所有行動，再加上：Everything at moderate, plus:

  • 傳送位置給家人Location is sent to family
  • 持續監測，不中斷Continuous monitoring
  • 依序聯絡緊急聯絡人，是否撥打 119 由家人決定Contacts are reached in order; the family decides whether to call 119

時間與門檻是規則 v1 的起始值，會與護理顧問一起調整。Times and thresholds are rule v1 starting values, to be tuned with our nursing advisor.

每個人都有自己的基準A baseline for each person

每個人的正常心率都不一樣。系統用過去兩週休息與睡眠時的資料，分別建立清醒與睡眠的基準；前 7 天先用保守的預設值。

Everyone’s normal heart rate is different. The system builds separate awake and asleep baselines from the last two weeks of rest and sleep, and uses conservative defaults for the first 7 days.

先排除運動Exercise is ruled out first

走路、運動時心率本來就會上升。活動結束 10 分鐘後，系統才重新判斷。

Heart rate naturally rises with walking and exercise. The system re-checks 10 minutes after the activity ends.

先觀察能不能恢復Wait for recovery

每一級行動之後都先觀察一段時間，沒有改善才升級，避免太多誤報讓家人疲乏。

After each level’s actions, the system waits to see whether things improve before escalating, so families aren’t worn down by false alarms.

依地區調整門檻Thresholds tuned by area

用 QGIS 分析都市熱島，替比較熱的地區調低環境門檻。

A QGIS urban heat-island map lowers the heat threshold in hotter neighbourhoods.

    系統架構Architecture

    一台伺服器，照顧許多長者One server, many elders

    樹莓派只負責量測、檢查、暫存與傳送，所有分析都在伺服器完成。每筆資料都帶著長者、節點、感測器、手錶與時間的編號，新增長者或設備只要登記，不用改程式。

    Each Raspberry Pi only measures, checks, buffers and sends. All analysis happens on the server. Every reading carries IDs for the elder, node, sensor, watch and time, so adding a person or a device is a registration with no code changes.

    斷網也不漏資料Nothing lost when Wi‑Fi drops

    每筆資料先寫進樹莓派本機，最多保存 7 天。恢復連線後依原始時間補傳，過去的時間不會再觸發舊警報。

    Every reading is saved on the Pi first and kept for up to 7 days. After reconnecting, it’s replayed with its original timestamp, and past minutes never set off stale alerts.

    設備只能代表自己Each device speaks only for itself

    每台樹莓派與每支手錶都有自己的憑證。伺服器用登記資料確認它屬於哪位長者，對不上的資料直接拒收。

    Every Pi and watch has its own credential. The server checks the registry to confirm which elder it belongs to and rejects anything that doesn’t match.

    加一位長者，不用改程式A new elder needs no new code

    新增長者、房間或感測器都只是登記一筆資料；手錶壞了，重新配對一支就好。

    Adding an elder, a room or a sensor is one registry entry. If a watch breaks, pair a new one.

    隱私與安全Privacy & safety

    先同意，才收集Consent comes first

    心率與位置都是敏感的個人資料。系統比照《個人資料保護法》的敏感資料標準處理：每個設備只能代表自己，每個家庭只看得到自己的長者。

    Heart rate and location are sensitive personal data, and the system handles them to the standard for sensitive data under Taiwan’s Personal Data Protection Act. Each device can speak only for itself, and each family sees only its own elder.

    同意後才啟用Nothing before consent

    長者與家人簽署同意書後才開始收集。生理資料、位置與冷氣控制分開同意，任何一項都能撤回。

    Collection starts only after the elder and family sign consent. Physiology, location and AC control are agreed to separately, and any of them can be withdrawn.

    冷氣連動隨時可關AC control can be switched off

    自動開冷氣可能和長者的省電習慣衝突，所以這項功能隨時可以關閉。

    Automatic AC can clash with an elder’s habit of saving power, so it can be switched off at any time.

    位置平時保持模糊Location stays coarse

    平時只保留大略位置；只有在中度以上的風險事件中，才顯示精確位置。

    Location is kept coarse day to day and shown precisely only during a moderate-or-higher risk event.

    只看得到自家長者Families see only their own

    每個家庭帳號只能看見自家長者的資料，每一次查看與操作都會留下紀錄。

    Each family account sees only its own elder’s data, and every view and command is logged.

    由人做最後決定People make the call

    AI 負責提醒與通知；要不要撥打 119，由家人決定。

    The AI informs and alerts. Whether to call 119 is the family’s decision.

    輔助照護，不做診斷Support, not diagnosis

    本系統不是醫療器材，也不做診斷。手錶量到的皮膚溫度不等於核心體溫。

    The system is not a medical device and does not diagnose. Skin temperature from a watch is not core body temperature.

    試辦與評估Pilot

    20 位長者，一個夏天Twenty elders, one summer

    試辦用三項指標檢驗成效。我們也會清楚說明限制：一個夏天裡真正的熱傷害事件可能不多，所以每一條規則都同時用情境重播測試驗證。

    Three measures test whether it works. We are also upfront about the limits: one summer may bring few real heat events, so every rule is also checked with scripted scenario replays.

    風險判斷準確度Accuracy of risk calls

    每一次中度以上的事件都由護理顧問審查是否合理，並比對家人回報、系統卻漏掉的事件。

    Our nursing advisor reviews every moderate-or-higher event, and we check for events families report that the system missed.
